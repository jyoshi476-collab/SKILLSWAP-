Learn Together. Grow Together.

SkillSwap is a student skill-sharing platform. Teach the skills you know and learn new skills from other students. Connect, collaborate and improve your knowledge together.

How SkillSwap Works

1. Create Your Profile

Register as a student and add the skills you can teach and the skills you want to learn.

2. Find Your Match

Discover students whose teaching skills match your learning interests.

3. Exchange Skills

Send a skill exchange request and learn from each other through collaboration.
